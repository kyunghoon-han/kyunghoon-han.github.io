Solution mode replaces ballistic gas flight with Langevin dynamics (solvent drag + thermal noise → molecules diffuse and are caged, as in water). Hydrophobicity adds a solvation free energy: burying nonpolar (C) surface on bonding is favourable (the hydrophobic effect) while burying polar (N,O) sites is penalised (they prefer to stay solvated). This enters reaction acceptance in both directions, so detailed balance holds on ΔG including solvation. Off = ideal gas.

Physical routes to persistence (from origin-of-life research). Wet–dry cycling (Damer–Deamer): the pool periodically dries — evaporating water suppresses hydrolysis and drives condensation, so bonds form and are kinetically trapped (Ross & Deamer 2016). Mineral surface (Ferris; Wächtershäuser): lowers barriers and — via bond stabilization — provides an adsorption free energy that stabilises condensed (amide/ester) bonds, so condensation is favoured and hydrolysis of a surface-bound bond is suppressed. This is what lets fragile molecules like amino acids persist instead of hydrolysing back the moment water returns (added as a free-energy factor, so energy is conserved and detailed balance holds). Outflow (chemostat/CSTR): washes out products so only species re-made fast enough survive. Spark discharge (Miller–Urey): rare lightning cracks N≡N to fix nitrogen. Chemical fuel (the thermodynamic drive): condensation in water is uphill, so an undriven box relaxes to a small-molecule equilibrium no matter how you tune it. Cyanamide (N≡C–NH₂), a real prebiotic condensing agent, activates an –OH into a leaving group; condensing it expels the very stable urea, and urea's stability pays for the new bond. Holding fuel above equilibrium and washing the waste out (a chemostat) parks the network at a driven non-equilibrium steady state. Every step has its explicit reverse (act/deact, acond/ahyd), so detailed balance of the elementary steps is untouched — the drive comes only from the fuel/waste reservoirs. Cut the fuel and complexity decays back: that collapse is the honest signature of a driven system, not a bug. To grow & keep amino acids: Reducing bath + Chemical fuel + Mineral surface + Wet–dry, T≈600–900 K, and a little Outflow so products don't just pile up.
