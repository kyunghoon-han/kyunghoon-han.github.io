H = ½(px² + py²) + ½(ω₁²x² + ω₂²y²) + λxy

2D coupled harmonic oscillator — the λxy term couples the two modes.

What am I looking at?

A simple example: two vibrational modes in the x and y directions, coupled by the term λxy. When one mode oscillates, it influences the other.

The "fat manifold" framework introduced in Chapter 5 of the thesis shows how this coupling manifests geometrically: instead of tracking the trajectory alone, we attach a perpendicular space at every point and study how that space twists and deforms along the path.

Configuration space trajectory γ(s)

Molecular motion path • current position in red • mode vectors in yellow/pink

Explanation

Each point along γ(s) represents a molecular configuration. The tangent vector (direction of motion) and the two perpendicular vectors at the current point form a local frame — these perpendicular directions are where the "fat" of the manifold lives.

Explanation

At each point along γ(s) we attach a perpendicular space showing possible vibrational displacements. Stacking these gives the tubular neighbourhood — the "fat" version of the curve. Watch the ellipses rotate and rescale as coupling acts.
